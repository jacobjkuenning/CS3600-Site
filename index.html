About Me

Hello! I am a Computer Science student at the University of Idaho. I am also a writer and a board game designer. I decided that I wanted to keep all of my projects in one place, and since Github Pages is free and I hate web programming, that's the purpose of this website.

Software

I started programming in high school by trying to make indie games. Eventually, in college, I realized that I disliked making games and became interested in lower level programming. I hope to one day get a job in embedded or systems level software.

More information about my software can be found here.

Board Games

Ever since my friend Sam showed me the deck-building game Dominion, I have been trying to capture the magic of a good game myself. I focus on making thinky two-player duelers with small form factors. The games I make are available via print-and-play, meaning I provide downloadables and instructions for making the game, and you assemble it. My favorite games are Innovation, Go, and Terraforming Mars.

You can learn more about my games here.
