Districts

Districts is a two player, abstract tile placement game where you play as competing City Officials. Players place tiles adjacent to or on top of existing tiles, matching together similar colored quadrants (called Districts) to create Sectors, claiming them. Each type of Sector has a unique ability that scales by the amount of Districts it contains. The player with the most controlled Districts at the end wins the game. Instructions on how to make and play Districts can be found here.

Quartum

Quartum is a two player, competitive matching game where players contribute to the same tableau of cards, trying to match 4 cards of a color. However, each player has two pieces that move around the tableau, changing the rules. Instructions on how to make and play Quartum can be found here.
